

Recipe from Nikesh Borkar

Ingredients

2 cups thick poha (flattened rice)
1 medium onion, finely chopped
1–2 green chillies, chopped
2 tbsp peanuts
½ tsp mustard seeds
½ tsp cumin seeds
8–10 curry leaves
½ tsp turmeric
1–2 tbsp oil
Salt to taste
½ tsp sugar (optional, but common in Maharashtrian poha)
½ lemon
Fresh coriander, chopped
1–2 tbsp grated coconut (optional)
👨‍🍳 Method
Wash the poha
Put poha in a strainer and rinse gently with water 1–2 times. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes. It should become soft but not mushy.
Season the poha
Add salt, turmeric and optional sugar to the softened poha. Mix gently.
Roast peanuts
Heat oil in a pan and fry peanuts until slightly crispy. Remove or push them to one side.
Make the tadka
Add mustard seeds. Once they crackle, add cumin seeds, curry leaves and green chillies.
Cook onion
Add chopped onion and sauté until it becomes slightly soft/translucent.
Add poha
Add the seasoned poha and mix gently so it doesn't break.
Cook
Cover and cook on low flame for 2–3 minutes.
Finish
Turn off the flame. Add lemon juice and coriander. Mix gently.
😋 Serve

Top with coriander, grated coconut and a little sev if you like.

Tip: For authentic Maharashtrian-style taste, don't overcook the poha. It should be soft, fluffy and separate, not sticky.
