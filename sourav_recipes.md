# Butter Chicken Recipe

Butter chicken, also known as Murgh Makhani, is a rich and creamy North Indian dish made with tender chicken in a spiced tomato-based gravy.

## Ingredients

### For Marinating the Chicken:
- 500g chicken (boneless or bone-in)
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

### For the Gravy:
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 large onion (finely chopped)
- 2 large tomatoes (pureed)
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup water (adjust for desired consistency)
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro (for garnish)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Marinate the Chicken
1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and lemon juice.
2. Add chicken pieces and coat them evenly with the marinade.
3. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight for best results.

### Step 2: Cook the Chicken
1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
2. Add the marinated chicken and cook until the chicken is browned and almost cooked through. Set aside.

### Step 3: Prepare the Gravy
1. In the same pan, add butter and cumin seeds. Let them crackle.
2. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
3. Stir in ginger-garlic paste and cook for another 1-2 minutes.
4. Add the tomato puree and cook until the oil separates from the gravy.
5. Add red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, ground coriander, and salt. Mix well.

### Step 4: Combine and Simmer
1. Add the cooked chicken to the gravy and stir to combine.
2. Pour in water and let the chicken simmer in the gravy for 10-15 minutes on low heat.
3. Crush kasuri methi between your palms and add it to the gravy.
4. Stir in the heavy cream and simmer for another 5 minutes.

### Step 5: Garnish and Serve
1. Garnish with fresh cilantro.
2. Serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed rice.

## Tips
- For a smokier flavor, you can char the chicken on a grill or in a broiler before adding it to the gravy.
- Adjust the level of cream and butter according to your preference for richness.
